## Stack

## Users

Manhattan residents, apartment occupants, office managers, and small-business owners who need a dependable cleaner and want to quickly understand the scope of work and request a quote.

## Product Purpose

Medina's Cleaning Services presents its cleaning work and gives prospective Manhattan clients a straightforward way to request an estimate.

## Positioning

Real, photo-documented cleaning transformations are the primary proof; the site should make the care in the finished spaces feel tangible without inventing unverified business claims.

## Operating Context

Visitors are often deciding from a phone between cleaning providers. They need to see work, understand typical service categories, confirm Manhattan coverage, and share enough details for a quote.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single-page marketing website with responsive navigation, service overview, photo gallery, and quote-request form.
- Service categories are presented as commonly requested scopes, not as a confirmed exhaustive menu.
- Contact phone, email, hours, prices, testimonials, and guarantees were not supplied and must not be fabricated.
- Form uses a clear front-end confirmation state; external form delivery is intentionally not assumed.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Medina's Cleaning Services.
- Location: Manhattan, New York.
- Real Facebook-post photos supplied by the user must be featured.
- BlackSheepPropertyMgmt is a UI inspiration reference, especially its confident editorial hierarchy and calm, service-led clarity.
